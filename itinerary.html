Plan Your Day

A Sample One-Day Itinerary

One way to hit the highlights without spending half your day in line.

Best time to go

General crowd-pattern logic that applies to most amusement parks, until we've gathered Lagoon-specific wait-time data:

Weekday vs. weekend

Tuesdays through Thursdays are consistently lighter than Friday–Sunday.

Opening vs. afternoon

Arriving at park opening lets you hit the 2-3 most popular rides (Primordial, Cannibal) before lines build.

Season

Late August through September (before Frightmares) tends to be quieter than peak summer.

The plan

Opening – 11:00am

Head straight to Primordial or Cannibal to beat the line before it builds.


11:00am – 1:00pm

Mid-morning rides, or Pioneer Village if it's typically less crowded during this window.


1:00pm – 2:00pm

Lunch — BYOF picnic or a stop at one of the food areas. See the food guide.


2:00pm – 5:00pm

Lagoon-A-Beach block if visiting in summer, or afternoon ride rotation.


5:00pm – close

Evening rides once crowds thin.
