Where to Stay Near Lagoon

Hotels, restaurants, and a few other things worth doing while you're in Farmington.

Closest hotels

Hampton Inn & Suites Salt Lake City/Farmington

The closest hotel to the park — close enough to see Lagoon from the parking lot. Free breakfast, indoor pool.

Hyatt Place Salt Lake City/Farmington/Station Park

Near Station Park's shopping and dining, a short drive from Lagoon.

Best Western Plus Layton Park Hotel

A short drive north, with an indoor pool and hot tub.

Candlewood Suites Layton

Extended-stay style rooms, good for longer trips.

Nearby restaurants

Most within a few minutes of the park at Station Park, Farmington's main shopping and dining district.

Twigs Bistro & Martini Bar

New American, upscale-casual, full bar.

Santorini's Greek Grill

Fresh Greek bowls and pitas — a multiple Best of State winner.

P.F. Chang's

Chinese, good for groups.

WildFin American Grill

Seafood-forward American menu.

Tucanos Brazilian Grill

Brazilian churrascaria-style dining.

Blaze Pizza / Cafe Zupas

Fast-casual options for a quick bite.

Other things to do in Farmington

Station Park

Farmington's main outdoor shopping center — 20+ restaurants, a Cinemark theater, and a fountain show.

Farmington Creek Trail

A scenic hiking/walking trail leading into the canyon, right near the park and campground.

Oakridge Country Club

A golf course nearby.

Antelope Island State Park

A short drive away on the Great Salt Lake — bison, hiking, and lake views.
