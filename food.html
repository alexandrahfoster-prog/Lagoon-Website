Eat Well, Spend Less

What to Eat at Lagoon

The best park food, plus the money-saving trick most visitors don't know about.

You can bring your own food

Lagoon has allowed outside food and drinks throughout nearly its entire history — a genuine rarity among major U.S. amusement parks. Pack a lunch, save your park-food budget for the one or two things worth trying.

Dietary notes

Allergies

Lagoon does not operate separate allergen-free kitchens — all products may contain or have been exposed to dairy, eggs, wheat, nuts, tree nuts, and shellfish.
