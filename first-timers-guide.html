The First-Timer's Guide to Lagoon

Everything you're probably wondering before your first trip — answered plainly, no fluff.

Before you go

When is Lagoon open?

Late March through the last Sunday in October, plus Frightmares weekends into early November.

How are tickets priced?

By height, not age. See the full pricing page for current numbers.

Do I need to book ahead?

Buying online ahead usually saves a few dollars and skips a ticket-window line.

What about parking?

Standard, preferred, and RV parking options are all available — see current rates.

You can bring your own food

Lagoon has allowed outside food and drinks throughout nearly its entire history — a genuine rarity among major U.S. amusement parks. Pack a cooler, plan a picnic lunch, and only buy park food for what you actually want to try. See the full food guide.

Questions every first-timer asks

Is Lagoon worth it if I've been to Disneyland?

They're different experiences — Lagoon is smaller and more affordable and has real vintage character. See the full comparison on our pricing page.


How long do we need — is one day enough?

One full day covers the rides comfortably. See our sample itinerary.


What should we absolutely not skip?

Primordial, Cannibal, and the 1921 Roller Coaster. Full breakdown on the rides page.
