Utah's Halloween Destination

Frightmares

Nine haunted attractions, two live shows, and a whole season's worth of Halloween content.

The basics

Dates

Runs weekends (and some weekdays) from early September through early November — check current-year dates.

Tickets

~$109.95 at the window, $5 off buying online. Season passport holders get discounted rates on add-ons.

Costumes

Not permitted on guests — no costumes, masks, or face paint (face painting is available inside the park).

Skip-the-line

A Time Warp Fast Pass covers short lines at the five main haunted houses: $30 Saturdays, $25 Thursday/Friday/Sunday, $2 off for season passport holders.

Haunted houses

Five main haunted walk-throughs make up the core scares (Lagoon markets "over nine" attractions total once kid-friendly options are counted).

Fun House of Fear

North Midway. "Strange, freakish and odd collide" — parental guidance suggested.

Nightmare Midway

North Midway. A house of illusion, described as too scary for most kids.

Frightening Frisco

Pioneer Village. New location for 2026 — an old-west ghost town overtaken by evil.

Malevolent Mansion

A mansion tour with spirits determined to scare you.

Nightwalk

One of the longer-running walk-throughs.

Live shows

Séance, the Dark Ritual

A fully immersive live show blending performance, physical effects, and audience participation, built around a witch and a cursed family. Separate ticket required: $17 ($15 for season passport holders). Purchase near the show entrance, northeast of the Game Time Arcade; tickets go on sale 45 minutes before the first showtime.

Hack and Slash

A chainsaw-themed scare show for older kids and adults — one of Lagoon's longest-running Frightmares entertainment traditions.

Kid-friendly Frightmares

Several "no scare zones" in Kiddieland keep the season fun for younger children.

Treat Street

A non-scary trick-or-treat trail through Lagoon-A-Beach, meeting friendly characters based on park rides.

Peter's Pumpkin Patch

Pumpkin and cookie decorating, games, and coloring — $15 extra ($13 for season passport holders).

Boolevard

A family-friendly Halloween photo area east of Colossus, open Friday–Sunday.
