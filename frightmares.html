Utah's Halloween Destination

Frightmares

Nine haunted attractions, two live shows, and a whole season's worth of Halloween content.

The basics

Dates

Weekends (and some weekdays) from early September through early November.

Tickets

~$109.95 at the window, $5 off online.

Costumes

Not permitted on guests — no costumes, masks, or face paint.

Skip-the-line

Time Warp Fast Pass: $30 Saturdays, $25 Thu/Fri/Sun.

Live shows

Séance, the Dark Ritual

A fully immersive live show built around a witch and a cursed family. Separate ticket: $17 ($15 for season passport holders).


Hack and Slash

A chainsaw-themed scare show for older kids and adults — one of Lagoon's longest-running Frightmares traditions.
