The Ride Guide

From a 1921 wooden coaster to a 2026 giant swing — here's what's worth the wait.

Primordial

Type: Interactive 3D dark coaster · Opened: 2023

A three-level roller coaster combined with an interactive dark ride inside a custom-built mountain — riders use onboard blasters and 3D glasses to battle through a fantasy storyline with multiple possible endings. Won the amusement industry's 2024 Brass Ring Award for best new ride.

Cannibal

Type: Steel launch coaster · Top speed: 70 mph

Lagoon's most intense ride — a 208-foot lift into a near-vertical freefall, an underground tunnel, and 3 inversions. Had the steepest drop of any coaster in North America until 2019.

Nutcracker

Type: Giant swing (S&S Screamin' Swing) · Opened: 2026

Lagoon's newest ride — a 105-foot steel structure swinging riders up to 135 feet, past vertical, at up to 68 mph. Sits on the old Cliffhanger site next to the Biergarten.

Roller Coaster

Type: Wooden coaster · Built: 1921

The 7th oldest operating roller coaster in the world, and one of only a few wooden coasters between Denver and the West Coast.

Kiddieland

The largest kiddie area of any U.S. amusement park — Baby Boats, BomBora, Puff the Little Fire Dragon, Red Baron, Scaliwags, Sky Fighter, and more.

North Midway

Cannibal, Primordial, Centennial Screamer, Double Thunder Raceway, Sky Coaster, Sky Scraper, Air Race, and the X-Venture Zone.

Park Entry Midway

Dracula's Castle and Terroride — Lagoon's two classic dark rides.

Pioneer Village

Rattlesnake Rapids, Rivets & Rotors, Steamworx, and Time Tinker — see the full Pioneer Village page.

South Midway

Wicked, Colossus: The Fire Dragon, Wild Mouse, The Spider, Merry-Go-Round, Nutcracker, and the historic Roller Coaster.

Lagoon-A-Beach

Slides, a lazy river, and open swimming — see the full water park page.

Rides you'll no longer find

Cliffhanger (removed after 2024, replaced by Nutcracker) and Jet Star II (retired November 2025 after nearly 50 years — parts are being reused in Farmington's planned All-Wheels skate park).
