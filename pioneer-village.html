Pioneer Village

A preserved slice of 19th-century Utah, tucked into the middle of the park.

A preserved slice of Utah history

Pioneer Village is a collection of relocated and preserved 19th-century Utah buildings and artifacts within the park. The oldest structure still standing in its original location anywhere on Lagoon's property — a small jail — dates to 1895. Nearby sits Lagoon's single oldest structure of any kind: a log cabin originally built in Ogden in 1851, predating Lagoon itself.

Photo Gallery & historic buildings

Beyond the rides and food, Pioneer Village includes a photo gallery and several preserved historic buildings worth a slower-paced walk-through — a good break between rides, especially with younger kids or older relatives in your group.
