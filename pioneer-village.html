Pioneer Village

A preserved slice of 19th-century Utah, tucked into the middle of the park.

A preserved slice of Utah history

Pioneer Village is a collection of relocated, preserved 19th-century Utah buildings and artifacts. The oldest structure still standing in its original location on Lagoon's property — a small jail — dates to 1895. Nearby sits Lagoon's single oldest structure of any kind: a log cabin originally built in Ogden in 1851.
