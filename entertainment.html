Live Entertainment at Lagoon

Magic, music, and a hidden speakeasy — all included with your ticket.

Smoke and Mirrors

Lagoon's signature magic show, staged at the Carousel Theater and free with admission. Presented in partnership with illusionist Joseph Wartnerchaney, the show blends large-scale illusions with live vocals and choreography in a different themed edition most seasons. Typically runs late May through mid-August — check current showtimes before you go.

Z's Emerald Underground

Lagoon's evening show, themed as a hidden speakeasy — live music, dance, and a nightlife-adjacent energy that's a change of pace from the daytime magic show. Also staged at the Carousel Theater and free with admission, typically running the same summer window as Smoke and Mirrors.

A history of live music at Lagoon

Live entertainment isn't new here — during the mid-20th century, Lagoon's stage hosted some of the biggest names in American big band and jazz music, part of an era when amusement parks doubled as regional concert venues. See the full history page.

New for 2026: The Atrium

Two adults-only (21+) social clubs — Carousel Club and Flirty Birdie — opened above Carousel Candy and Peacock Parlour, offering mocktails, cocktails, and small bites. Open daily from noon. A different kind of "entertainment" than the stage shows, but worth knowing about if you're planning an adults' evening at the park.

Seasonal entertainment

During Frightmares, the regular summer shows are replaced with Halloween-specific entertainment, including an immersive live show and a chainsaw-themed scare show. See the full Frightmares page.
