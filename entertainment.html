Live Entertainment at Lagoon

Magic, music, and a hidden speakeasy — all included with your ticket.

Smoke and Mirrors

Lagoon's signature magic show, staged at the Carousel Theater and free with admission — large-scale illusions with live vocals and choreography, presented with illusionist Joseph Wartnerchaney. Typically runs late May through mid-August.

Z's Emerald Underground

Lagoon's evening show, themed as a hidden speakeasy — live music and dance. Also at the Carousel Theater, free with admission, running the same summer window as Smoke and Mirrors.

A history of live music at Lagoon

During the mid-20th century, Lagoon's stage hosted some of the biggest names in American big band and jazz music. See the full history page.

New for 2026: The Atrium

Two adults-only (21+) social clubs — Carousel Club and Flirty Birdie — opened above Carousel Candy and Peacock Parlour, offering mocktails, cocktails, and small bites. Open daily from noon.

Seasonal entertainment

During Frightmares, the regular summer shows are replaced with Halloween-specific entertainment. See the full Frightmares page.
