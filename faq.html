Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

The questions every first-timer asks, answered in one place.

Is Lagoon worth it if I've been to Disneyland?

They're genuinely different experiences — Lagoon is smaller and cheaper per visit, lets you bring your own food, and has real vintage character. See the full Lagoon vs. Disneyland breakdown.


How long do we need — is one day enough?

One day covers the rides comfortably. If you're also doing Lagoon-A-Beach, arrive at opening.


What should we absolutely not skip?

Primordial and Cannibal for thrill-seekers, the 1921 Roller Coaster for the history, and Pioneer Village for a slower-paced break.


Can we really bring our own food?

Yes — Lagoon has allowed outside food throughout nearly its entire history. See the food guide.


How much does parking cost?

Standard is $20, preferred is $35, RV/oversized is $30. Full pricing on the pricing page.


Are tickets priced by age or height?

By height — see the pricing page for current thresholds.


Is Lagoon a good spot for a family reunion?

Yes — with discounted group rates and a reservable terrace. Details on the pricing page.


What's the deal with Frightmares?

Lagoon's Halloween event runs September into early November, with haunted houses, an immersive show, and non-scary kids' attractions. See the Frightmares page.


Can we stay overnight nearby?

Yes — an on-site RV park and campground, plus several nearby hotels. See RV & Campground and Where to Stay.
