Family reunions & group events

Groups of 15+ (including reunions and community events) qualify for a discounted rate, typically bringing the per-ticket price down to around $77–79 with a promo code. A reservable terrace is available for a $50 fee. Contact Lagoon at least two weeks ahead — the Terraces area (Cedar, Juniper, Birch) is a popular reunion spot.

How Lagoon compares to Disneyland

Disneyland's single-day ticket uses demand-based pricing ranging from about $104 to $224, before parking (~$30+) and food. Lagoon charges one flat rate (~$99.95–109.95) regardless of date, with $20 standard parking and the option to bring your own food entirely — a real cost advantage, even before Disney's optional Lightning Lane add-on (~$34/person/day). Lagoon is smaller and less elaborately themed, but for pure per-visit affordability, it's a meaningfully cheaper day.
