Tickets & Pricing

What everything actually costs, in one place — plus how it stacks up against Disneyland.

Single-day tickets: Frightmares 2026

Lagoon now uses dynamic, date-based pricing for single-day tickets. The price depends on the day you pick: busy Saturdays and fall break cost the most, and Friday evenings and the last weekend cost the least. Prices below are Lagoon's online prices. At the ticket window, every day is $109.95.

Youth pricing is based on height, not age. On the cheapest days, everyone pays the same price.

Season passport prices over the years

Lagoon doesn't raise prices every single year. It tends to bump the price, hold it for a season or two, then bump it again. Over the last decade, though, the trend is clearly up: the Black Friday sale price has nearly doubled. Since 2026 matched 2025, another increase for 2027 seems likely.

Family reunions & group events

Lagoon offers group tickets for groups of 25 or more, including reunions and community events. Lagoon hasn't posted its group ticket prices yet (its site lists them as "TBD"). Groups can also reserve a terrace for a $50 reservation fee, first come, first served. The Terraces area (Cedar, Juniper, Birch) is a popular reunion spot. See Lagoon's group page for current details.

How Lagoon compares to Disneyland

Both parks now use date-based pricing, so the day you pick matters at either one. Lagoon's online tickets this fall run $69.95 to $104.95, with $20 standard parking, and you can bring your own food and drink into the park. That last part alone can save a family a lot compared with a Disneyland day. Lagoon is smaller and less elaborately themed, but for pure per-visit cost, it's a meaningfully cheaper day.
