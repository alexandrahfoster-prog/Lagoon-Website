Family reunions & group events

Lagoon is a genuinely popular reunion and group-event venue — an underserved topic online given how often it happens.

Groups of 15+ (including family reunions and community events) qualify for a discounted rate, typically bringing the per-ticket price down to around $77–79 with a promo code. A reservable terrace is available for a $50 fee. Contact Lagoon directly at least two weeks ahead of your date to set one up — the Terraces area (Cedar, Juniper, and Birch Terrace) is a popular reunion spot near the north end of the park.

How Lagoon compares to Disneyland

Disneyland's single-day, single-park ticket uses demand-based pricing that ranges from about $104 to $224 depending on the date, before parking (~$30+) and food. Lagoon charges one flat rate (~$99.95–109.95 depending on season) regardless of which day you visit, with $20 standard parking and the option to bring your own food entirely — a real cost advantage for a family of four, even before factoring in Disney's optional Lightning Lane skip-the-line add-on (roughly $34/person/day). Lagoon is smaller and doesn't have Disney's scale of theming, but for pure per-visit affordability, it's a meaningfully cheaper day.
