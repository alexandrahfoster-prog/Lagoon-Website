Work at Lagoon for Free Season Passes: The Secret Job for Moms (and Anyone 18+)

While the teenage crew is back in school, Lagoon needs grown-ups. Work 10 shifts and your family rides all of 2027 for free. You have two chances to earn them:

  • Fall 2026 (post-season, Sept 25 – Nov 8) → four 2027 Season Passports
  • Spring 2027 (pre-season) → four 2027 Season Passports

By Sasha FosterUpdated September 23, 20268 min read

The quick version

  • Who: Anyone 18 or older who isn't already a Lagoon employee: moms, dads, grandparents, retirees, college students.
  • The deal: Work 10 shifts and 60 hours, earn $10/hour plus four 2027 Season Passports.
  • When (fall 2026): Any operating day Sept 25 – Nov 8, 2026, with at least 2 shifts on Oct 15–18. Earns 2027 passes.
  • Or (spring 2027): The pre-season program also earns 2027 Season Passports. Dates not posted yet.
  • The catch: Miss one scheduled shift without calling and you lose all the passes.

Heads up: The 2026 post-season window opens Friday, September 25, and shifts are first come, first served. If you want in, apply this week.

Every Utah parent knows the moment. You add up four Lagoon tickets, parking, and a round of Lagoon-a-Beach snacks, and suddenly a "fun family day" costs more than a car payment. So it feels a little too good to be true that Lagoon will pay you and hand your family four Season Passports if you work a handful of fall evenings.

It's real, it's been around for years, and plenty of Davis County families swear by it. Here's how it works, what the fine print says, and how to make sure you actually walk away with those passes.

Why Lagoon hires adults in spring and fall

Most of Lagoon's summer staff are high school students. That works well from June through August, but in spring and fall those students are in class during the day and busy with homework, sports and dances at night. Meanwhile Lagoon is still open, and in the fall it's packed for Frightmares.

To fill the gap, Lagoon runs pre-season (spring) and post-season (fall) Temporary Staff programs for adults. Locals have long called these workers the "School Day Moms." Back in 2014 the Standard-Examiner reported that about 350 temporary workers were helping out that spring, and that the group included plenty of dads, grandparents, aunts and uncles too.

"The kids bring me back every year." A longtime School Day Mom, quoted in the Standard-Examiner (2014)

This fall's version is officially called Post-Season Temporary Staff: Weekend Warrior, and it's open to applicants 18 and older.

Lagoon also ran an Option #1 this year (Aug 17 – Sept 28, with shorter 5–10 p.m. shifts). It's nearly over, but keep it in mind for next August.

The jobs: ride operator or food service

Temporary Staff are placed where Lagoon needs them most, usually in one of two roles.

🎢

Ride Operator

Load and unload guests, check restraints, run the control panel, give safety instructions, and watch the ride while it runs. You may rotate between rides, so expect to learn several.

🍟

Food Service

Prep and serve food at the concession stands or the Group Foods catering area, handle cash and make change, and keep things clean. You'll need a Davis County Food Handler's Permit.

Both jobs are physical. The posting asks that you can stand and walk for up to eight hours, work outside in whatever weather October brings, and stay calm with loud rides and big crowds around you. Ride operators should also be comfortable crouching, kneeling and stooping.

What the schedule really looks like

These are evening shifts. Plan to start between 2:00 and 5:00 p.m. and stay until the park closes and your area is cleaned. During Frightmares that usually means:

  • Fridays and Saturdays: the park closes at 11 p.m., so you'll likely finish close to midnight.
  • Sundays: the park closes at 8 p.m.
  • Oct 15–18: the park runs extended days (10 a.m. – 11 p.m. Thursday through Saturday), which is why Lagoon requires two shifts that weekend. Many Utah schools are on fall break then, and the park is packed.

Always check Lagoon's official operating calendar for exact closing times before you pick shifts.

Is it worth it? The math

Here's roughly what one family gets for hitting the 60-hour minimum:

Total value for your family$1,500+
$227.95 is the current price on Lagoon's Season Pass site, before taxes and fees, and a pass bought today only lasts until the park closes for the year in early November. The price was the same in 2025 and 2026, and Lagoon has raised Season Passport prices several times in recent years (see our price history), so 2027 passes will likely cost even more.

Our take: $10 an hour isn't the point. The passports are. For a family that already goes to Lagoon a few times a summer, a few weeks of fall evenings is one of the best deals in Utah.

The fine print you can't skip

This is where people lose their passes. Read it twice.

  • One no-show and you lose everything. If you skip a scheduled shift without calling, or end up short on shifts or hours, you forfeit all incentive passports, not just some.
  • Once it's on the schedule, it's yours. Shifts are picked in advance with a scheduling manager, first come, first served. Once you pick a shift, you're responsible for it.
  • Weather cancellations can still count. If Lagoon cancels your shift with less than 24 hours' notice or sends you home early, you get credit for one shift and up to 6 hours (less any hours you were paid for). If they cancel 24+ hours ahead, you get no credit, so you'll need to pick up another shift.
  • Passes are for your family only. They have no cash value, can't be extended, and can't be sold. Selling them can get you fired or even prosecuted.
  • No other employee perks. Temporary Staff aren't eligible for end-of-season bonuses or Employee Appreciation Days, and may be excluded from other benefits.
  • Terms can change. Lagoon reserves the right to change the program at any time, so confirm details with Employee Services.

Tips to earn every pass

  1. Sign up for 11 or 12 shifts, not 10. A buffer protects you if a shift gets canceled a day ahead or you get cut early and come in under 60 hours.
  2. Grab your Oct 15–18 shifts first. They're required, and they're the busiest days of the fall.
  3. Line up childcare before you commit. Late nights are the hardest part for parents. Swap nights with a spouse, grandparent or neighbor.
  4. Get your food handler's permit early if you might be placed in food service. It's quick to do online through Davis County.
  5. Dress in layers. Farmington evenings in late October can drop into the 30s. Warm socks and broken-in shoes will save your night.
  6. Sign up with a friend. Plenty of School Day Moms carpool together, and a familiar face makes a 7-hour shift go faster.
  7. Track your hours yourself. Keep a simple note on your phone of every shift and hour so you know exactly where you stand.

What about the spring pre-season?

Lagoon runs a similar pre-season program in spring, when the park opens for the year but high schoolers are still in class. It's historically been the classic "School Day Moms" gig, with daytime shifts that can fit around school drop-off and pickup. If you miss this fall's window, spring 2027 is your second shot at four 2027 Season Passports, since you'd earn them right as the season starts. Details and dates for 2027 haven't been posted yet. Watch Lagoon's Temporary Staff page early in the year, because spots go fast.

How to apply

  1. Check the official page. Go to lagoonpark.com/jobs/post-season-help to confirm this season's options and dates.
  2. Apply online. Click "Apply" to reach Lagoon's hiring portal and choose the Post-Season Temporary Staff: Weekend Warrior (18+) posting.
  3. Schedule your shifts. Once you're hired, work with the scheduling manager to lock in at least 10 shifts, including two on Oct 15–18.
  4. Show up and track your hours. Finish every scheduled shift, hit 60 hours, and your 2027 passports are earned.

Questions? Call Lagoon Employee Services at (801) 451-8058.

Frequently asked questions

Can adults work at Lagoon part time?

Yes. Lagoon's pre-season (spring) and post-season (fall) Temporary Staff programs are built for adults 18 and older, covering shifts while the regular high school workforce is in class. Current Lagoon employees can't join.

How many Season Passports can I earn?

For the 2026 post-season Weekend Warrior program, you can earn four 2027 Season Passports by working at least 10 shifts and 60 hours between Sept 25 and Nov 8, 2026, including two shifts on Oct 15–18.

How much does Lagoon pay temporary staff?

$10.00 per hour in 2026, plus the Season Passport incentive.

Do I have to be a mom?

Not at all. "School Day Moms" is just a nickname. Dads, grandparents, retirees, college students and anyone else 18+ can apply.

What happens if I get sick and miss a shift?

Call in. A no call/no show forfeits all your passes. If you do miss a shift, talk to your scheduling manager right away about picking up another one so you still hit 10 shifts and 60 hours.

Can I sell or give away the passes?

No. They're for your own family only, have no cash value, and can't be sold. Selling them can lead to termination or prosecution.

Do temporary staff get other employee perks?

Generally no. Temporary Staff aren't eligible for end-of-season bonuses or Employee Appreciation Days and may be excluded from other employee benefits.
