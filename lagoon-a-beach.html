Lagoon's Water Park

Lagoon-A-Beach

Nine named slides, a lazy river, and open swimming — here's what's actually there.

Slides & towers

The Pipeline

Four body/tube slides: Shout, Twist, Pipeline, and Wipeout.

Serpentine Slides

Three tube slides — Back Door, Rip Curl, Sea Snake — reaching 56 feet.

Speed Slides

Liquid Lightning and The Drop — the tallest slides at 65 feet.

The Outrigger

A tube slide with a series of pools along the way.

Advertisement — inline slot

Pools & the lazy river

Mooch's Mainstream

A lazy river — 234,000 gallons across nearly 11,000 sq ft, 4 ft deep.

Bongo's Bay

A water playground for younger kids.

Blue Lagoon

Open swimming with coves and waterfalls.

Facilities

The Beach House

Lockers, changing rooms, and restrooms.

Rad Brad's Surf Rags

Swimwear and gear shop at the entrance.

Ruby's Tube Rentals

Rent a tube rather than bring your own.

Volleyball Courts & Sun Deck

Sand court and an open deck overlooking the water park.

Good to know

Lagoon-A-Beach is not open during Frightmares. Bring water shoes if you're sensitive to hot pavement.
